Mitchell Craft

Creative Technology · AI + 3D Production
Based in New York City

I’m a creative technologist at Google on YouTube’s Effects team. I build interactive effects, creative tools and AI-assisted production workflows, bringing together Blender, real-time graphics, generative models and code.

I co-founded INCworks Studio, with commercial work for Miu Miu, Moncler, Elena Velez, Megan Thee Stallion and Bring Me The Horizon. My background also includes technical art pipelines, artist-facing tools, and teaching digital art at Tulane University.

My current practice includes Blender MCP automation, agent workflows with validation and failure handling, local FLUX LoRA training, API-driven generative video, and a mobile-first 3D painting app in Three.js. I’m also co-founding Provenance, an AI platform with a multi-step model pipeline and a verification layer.
